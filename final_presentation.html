Definition and typology

Definition

  • ICAO is a specialized agency of the United Nations.
  • Its Assembly meets every three years.
  • Its headquarters are in Montreal, Canada.
  • It has 193 Member States.

03 · One lesson

Effect does not necessarily require power.

ICAO can influence aircraft safety without directly certifying or operating an aircraft.

04 · One suggestion

Extend the standards to space travel.

ICAO should begin developing an international aviation-to-space safety framework before commercial space travel becomes routine.
